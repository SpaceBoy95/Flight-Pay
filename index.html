Cross-check payslip

Enter the category totals shown on your CVP supplementary payslip for the month you're checking. This compares them against what the app calculated from your logged entries.

Import old payslips

Open the payslip PDF, copy the whole VARIABLE PAY section, and paste it below. Sector pay imports with route and amount worked out automatically; standby and day-off lines import as placeholders since the payslip doesn't show those amounts per line, you'll want to fill those in from the TOTALS section.

Import delay history from CSV

For a CSV export (e.g. from Flighty or your own log). Pick the file, then tell the app which columns hold the date and delay — either a ready-made delay-minutes column, or the scheduled and actual arrival times, and the delay is worked out for you. This only logs delay evidence, no pay is attached.

Pay deals

Each pay deal applies from its effective date onwards. Entries always use whichever deal was in force on their own date, so adding a new deal for a pay rise never changes what past entries worked out to.
